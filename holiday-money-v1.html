How the signal works

Each rate is ranked against its own history. The score blends where today sits in the last five years (60%) with where it sits across all available data (40%). 75+ is a great time to buy, below 40 is below average.

All-time high and low

ATH and ATL are the highest and lowest monthly closing rates in the data: from Oct 2007 for most currencies, and from Dec 2014 for KRW, THB, IDR, VND and PHP. For two non-USD currencies, history starts where both have data.

Before you exchange

These are mid-market rates. Banks, airports and cards add a margin of 1–10%, so compare what you are actually offered. This is general information, not financial advice.
